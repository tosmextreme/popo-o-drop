How to Play

🎯 The Goal

Drop shapes into the bowl. When two of the same kind touch, they merge into the next tier. Keep merging until you make the rainbow flower!

🎮 Controls

  • Move mouse to aim, click to drop
  • Hold A / D to tilt the bowl left/right
  • Press Space to shake the bowl

💖 Lives & Scoring

  • You have 5 drops — if 5 shapes fall off the bowl, game over
  • Every 10,000 points = +1 extra life
  • Combo merges quickly for x2, x3, x4... multipliers
  • Make the rainbow flower → tap it to pop for +1000 bonus!

🌟 Stage 2 (Unlock at 5K)

  • 2–2.5× score on everything
  • The bowl is a balancing scale — tilt to keep it level
  • Wider, flared rim makes objects easier to lose
  • 💣 Beware of bombs! 3 drops after appearing they explode and knock everything around
  • No going back to Stage 1 until game over

🌠 Stage 3 (Unlock at 15K)

  • 3–3.5× score on everything
  • 🌙 Gravity flips every 8–15 drops with countdown warnings
  • Don't shake during the countdown!
  • ☄ Asteroids telegraph their entry with an arrow before crashing in
  • Two asteroids that collide cancel each other out
  • Bombs from Stage 2 are still around

💡 Tips

  • Save shake for stuck piles — it's free to use
  • Tilt stamina drains fast — short bursts work better
  • Newly dropped shapes fall straight even when tilted
